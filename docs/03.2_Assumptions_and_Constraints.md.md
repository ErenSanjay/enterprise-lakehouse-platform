# 1. Purpose

This document captures the key assumptions, constraints, risks, dependencies, and unresolved questions identified before the solution architecture is finalized.

These inputs define the architectural boundaries within which the Enterprise Lakehouse Platform will be designed and implemented.

This document intentionally avoids technology-specific decisions. Technology selections are documented separately in the Architecture Decision Records (ADR) and High-Level Design documents.

---

# 2. Business Assumptions

The following business assumptions have been agreed upon for Phase 1 of the project.

---

## BA-001 Daily Data Availability

Business applications will publish data according to agreed delivery schedules.

Late deliveries are expected to be exceptional rather than routine.

---

## BA-002 Data Ownership

Operational systems remain the authoritative owners of business data.

The Lakehouse Platform is responsible only for ingestion, transformation, validation, and analytical consumption.

---

## BA-003 Reporting Schedule

Business users require refreshed analytical data before the beginning of each business day.

Daily reporting is considered the minimum business expectation.

---

## BA-004 Business Growth

Additional business domains and source systems will be onboarded after the initial implementation.

The platform should support expansion without major redesign.

---

## BA-005 Historical Data

Historical datasets required for analytics will be available before the initial production deployment.

---

# 3. Technical Assumptions

The following technical assumptions influence the architecture but do not prescribe specific implementation technologies.

---

## TA-001 Structured Source Data

Source systems primarily provide structured or semi-structured datasets.

---

## TA-002 Stable Connectivity

Reliable network connectivity exists between source systems, storage, and processing environments.

---

## TA-003 Consistent Data Formats

Source systems publish data using agreed schemas and supported exchange formats.

Minor schema evolution is expected over time.

---

## TA-004 Independent Compute and Storage

The architecture should support independent scaling of storage and compute resources.

---

## TA-005 Recoverable Processing

Data processing should support restart and recovery without requiring complete reprocessing.

---

# 4. Operational Assumptions

---

## OA-001 Scheduled Processing

Data processing occurs according to predefined schedules.

Continuous event-driven processing is not required during the initial implementation.

---

## OA-002 Environment Availability

Development, testing, and production environments are available throughout the project lifecycle.

---

## OA-003 Operational Support

Operations teams are responsible for monitoring scheduled jobs and responding to production incidents.

---

## OA-004 Monitoring

Pipeline execution metrics, logs, and operational events are continuously collected and retained.

---

## OA-005 Change Management

All production deployments follow organizational change management and release approval processes.

---

# 5. Constraints

The following constraints define the boundaries of Phase 1.

---

## C-001 Source System Independence

Operational source systems cannot be modified solely to support analytical workloads.

---

## C-002 Processing Scope

Phase 1 supports scheduled batch processing only.

Real-time and streaming workloads are outside the current scope.

---

## C-003 Supported Data Types

The initial implementation focuses on structured and semi-structured business data.

Unstructured document processing is excluded.

---

## C-004 Deployment Scope

Only Development, QA, and Production environments are included.

Additional environments require future approval.

---

## C-005 Delivery Timeline

The initial release must prioritize core analytical capabilities over advanced platform features.

---

## C-006 Security Compliance

The platform must comply with organizational security and governance policies.

---

# 6. Risks

The following risks have been identified during project planning.

| Risk ID | Risk | Potential Impact | Mitigation |
|----------|------|-----------------|------------|
| R-001 | Delayed source data delivery | Reporting delays | Retry mechanisms and alerting |
| R-002 | Schema evolution | Pipeline failures | Flexible schema handling |
| R-003 | Poor source data quality | Incorrect business metrics | Validation framework |
| R-004 | Rapid data growth | Performance degradation | Scalable architecture |
| R-005 | Infrastructure outages | Missed processing windows | Recovery procedures and monitoring |
| R-006 | Increasing business complexity | Longer development cycles | Modular platform design |

# 9. Summary

The assumptions and constraints documented here establish the boundaries for designing the Enterprise Lakehouse Platform.

These assumptions guide architectural decisions while ensuring the platform remains aligned with business expectations, operational practices, and organizational governance.

Technology selections, deployment architecture, and implementation details are intentionally excluded from this document and are defined in subsequent architecture and design documents.
